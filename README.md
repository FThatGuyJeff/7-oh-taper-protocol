# 7-OH Taper Protocol

A harm-reduction method for tapering off **7-OH (7-hydroxymitragynine)** — the concentrated kratom extract — by using an AI as an accountability partner to report doses to, set goals with, and log your intake, then stepping down from 7-OH to plain dry-leaf kratom and finally off completely.

> **What's 7-OH?** 7-hydroxymitragynine is the much more potent, concentrated alkaloid in kratom extract shots. If you've been using 7-OH extracts, this guide is written for you — but note one critical rule up front: **do not switch to a 7-OH extract mid-taper.** It's the reverse of tapering and re-spikes tolerance.

> **⚠️ I am not a medical professional, and this is not medical advice.** This is a self-directed accountability method — a way to use an AI to report doses, set goals, and log your intake so the taper stays honest and on track. It is not a substitute for a doctor. **Consult a medical professional whenever needed**, and seek emergency care for the red-flag symptoms listed below.

## Core principles

1. **Step down slowly.** Reduce your daily total by roughly one dose per day (e.g. 12 → 11 → 10 → … → 1 → 0). Hold flat on hard days — the ladder tolerates pause days.
2. **Bridge with plain leaf, never extract.** Use plain dry-leaf capsules to ease withdrawal between reductions. Never switch to a concentrated extract shot (7-OH) mid-taper — that is the reverse of tapering and re-spikes tolerance.
3. **Log everything.** Every dose, with time and amount. The record is what makes the taper measurable and keeps you honest.
4. **Never chase the number.** If you're under target, that's good — you're tapering. Under is fine; over is what quietly corrupts the plan.

## The method in practice

### Step-down schedule
Pick a starting daily total and reduce by a fixed increment each day. Reassess on rough days and hold flat rather than forcing a step down.

### Capsule bridge
Between reductions, plain-leaf capsules carry the gap. Take the *minimum* that holds function, spaced at least **30 minutes** apart so one dose peaks before the next is added. Stacking doses on an un-peaked dose wastes material and taxes your stomach without buying relief.

### Powder-ration titration (the final stretch)
When the remaining daily amount gets hard to split into consistent pieces, crush the day's ration into powder and divide it into even lines. Take one line per gap (90+ minutes). The shrinking line count makes the finish visible.

### The 30-minute wait test
When a craving or withdrawal spike hits, wait 30 minutes.
- Still symptomatic after 30 → real need, take it.
- Faded → habit, bank the win.

This is how you tell physical need apart from the dosing habit — and it's the single most useful tool in the final stretch.

### Watch your stomach (the GI-load ceiling)
Dry-leaf powder is raw plant fiber. Large daily totals (roughly 40+ capsules/day) can cause nausea and vomiting — that is the real ceiling, not the alkaloid math. Goal: **fewest effective capsules, spaced.** Eat before your first dose of the day; empty-stomach stacking is what brings on nausea.

If you vomit, the amount you *took* overstates what actually *absorbed* — anchor your next day's number to what you could hold down, not what you swallowed.

### Hydration is non-negotiable
Withdrawal sweats plus any vomiting together create dehydration risk. Small sips of water/electrolytes frequently, not gulps. If you can't keep water down for several hours, that is a seek-medical-care sign, not a push-through.

## Safety red flags — get medical help if you experience:

- Inability to keep fluids down for several hours
- Signs of severe dehydration (dizziness, dark urine, little or no urination)
- Chest pain, fainting, or irregular heartbeat
- Thoughts of self-harm (US: call or text **988**)
- Symptoms that are severe, unusual, or getting worse

---

## What NOT to do

- Don't switch to concentrated extracts (7-OH shots) mid-taper.
- Don't stack doses back-to-back without the 30-minute wait.
- Don't white-knuckle a dangerous symptom instead of getting medical care.
